Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Head2Hoops ("we", "us") is a free website for comparing NBA player statistics. This policy explains what information is collected when you use head2hoops.com, who collects it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • We don't ask for accounts, names, emails or any personal information.
  • Your browser stores recently viewed player data on your own device so pages load faster.
  • Our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs.
  • We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, in aggregate.
  • We show ads from Google, which may use cookies to personalise ads. You can opt out.

Information stored on your device

To make the site faster, your browser saves the player list and recently viewed player stats in its local storage for up to 24 hours. This data stays on your device, contains only public basketball statistics, and is never sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on Amazon Web Services (Amazon S3 and CloudFront). Like most websites, AWS may automatically record technical information when your browser requests a page, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and troubleshoot problems. See the AWS Privacy Notice.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages and players are viewed, which features are used, roughly where visitors are located (country or city level), and what device and browser they use. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect this information, and we only look at it in aggregate. IP addresses are not logged or stored by Google Analytics 4. We do not use it to identify you.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland do not receive analytics cookies unless they consent. You can also opt out everywhere with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Third-party resources

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and a charting library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). When your browser downloads these files, those providers receive your IP address and browser information, as they would for any website using them. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements, which keeps the site free. Google and its partners are third-party vendors that use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalised advertising cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time through the consent message.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal information, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for advertising. Because we don't collect personal information ourselves, most requests are best handled directly through the providers above, using the opt-out links in this policy. You can also contact us and we'll help.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sources

Statistics are compiled from publicly available NBA data. Head2Hoops is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NBA or its teams.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
